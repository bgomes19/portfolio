About

Design with
purpose.

I'm a Computer Science student at MacEwan University who designs interfaces that make sense for the people using them.

I think good design isn't just about how something looks. It's about whether it works, feels right, and solves a real problem. I approach every project by understanding the goal first, then building something around that. Whether it's a mobile app, a landing page, or a brand identity, the process is the same: clarity first, aesthetics second.
